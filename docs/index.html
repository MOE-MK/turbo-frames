Open-source carbon FPV frames

TURBO35

A 3.5-inch frame for freestyle and cinematic flying, cut from T700 carbon. Built for durability, with no props in view. Download it and build it yourself.

T35 Plates

5 mm unibody bottom.

The bottom plate is one continuous 5 mm piece of T700 carbon, paired with a 3 mm top plate. The shape is rounded everywhere, with no sharp angles, to lower the chance of a break in hard landings and crashes.

T35 Electronics

Stack it your way.

Mount a 25×25 AIO, a 20×20 stack or a 30×30 stack. There's room for the video transmitter too, including the DJI O3.

Every mounting pattern on the bottom plate, lit.

T35 Power

9×9 or 12×12 motors.

Slotted motor mounts take both bolt patterns. The frame is designed around 16xx–18xx motors at 3000–4000 KV, on a 4S 850–1000 mAh LiPo.

The blue rings mark the 9 mm and 12 mm bolt circles each slot covers.

T35 Radio

Antenna slot, built in.

A slot at the back of the top plate locks a whip antenna in place, so you don't need a 3D-printed mount for it. Tested and proven.

T35 Assembled

No props in view.

The props stay out of the camera's view for a clean, uninterrupted feed. Ten M2 × 25 mm standoffs hold the plates together. M2 is on purpose, so the standoffs can absorb hits.
