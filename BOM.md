# Bill of Materials

Hardware counts below come from the hole patterns in the DXFs. Choose standoff and screw lengths to fit your stack height.

## Turbo35

| Qty | Part | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Bottom plate | 5 mm T700 carbon, `dxf/Turbo35_Bottom.dxf` |
| 1 | Top plate | 3 mm T700 carbon, `dxf/Turbo35_Top.dxf` |
| 7 | M2 standoffs | Plate-to-plate |
| 14 | M2 screws | For the standoffs |
| 16 | M2 motor screws | 4 per motor |
| 4 | M3 screws / stack hardware | Only for a 30×30 stack (these holes are M3) |

**Hole reference (bottom plate)**
- Motor mounts: slotted, fit 9×9 and 12×12 motor patterns (M2)
- Center stack: 20×20 (M2), 30.5×30.5 (M3), 25.5×25.5 rotated 45° (M2)
- Rear mounting area: 20×20 and 25.5×25.5 (M2)
- Antenna: slot at the back of the top plate
